# Talent Brief Style Guide

## Core Shape

Write a compact Chinese paragraph that a talent teammate can paste into a founder/HRD hiring group.

Default structure:

```text
推荐一位有<总年限>工作经验的<职能方向>候选人，目前在<当前公司/公司类型>负责<明确职责>；此前在<可公开公司>支持<具体业务/团队/项目>，<一至两条目标相关能力或资源>。<用户补充的简短评价>，open 看<方向/角色>机会，希望 base<城市>，感兴趣可以私信～
```

For students and recent graduates, use a cohort-led structure:

```text
推荐一位<毕业届别>的同学，<硕士院校/专业>、<本科院校/专业>，目前在<当前公司类型>任<岗位>，负责<明确职责>；毕业前有过<目标相关经历类别>，目前<状态>，open 看<接收群体可承接的角色>，优先 base<城市>，感兴趣可以私信～
```

Default to `推荐一位...` without the candidate's name. Add a name only when the user asks for a named message.

If total years or segment durations are unavailable, use this shorter opening instead:

```text
推荐一位<职能方向>候选人，曾在<公司/行业>负责<明确职责>，目前在<当前公司类型>负责<明确职责>；...
```

Do not replace missing dates with `多年经验`、`长期深耕`、`资深`. Do not combine an employer from one statement with a role from another unless the source explicitly links them.

## Generic Or Targeted

- Generic group brief: cover the candidate's strongest overall background and open direction.
- Specific company/JD: reorder the paragraph around that target's hiring problem. Put matching domain experience, team exposure, delivery record, and supported talent resources before less relevant achievements.
- If the notes imply a specific portfolio referral but the target or role is unclear, keep the generic draft and add one `建议补问` line rather than guessing.
- Match the open direction to the recipients. In a portfolio founder/HRD group, keep startup roles and omit pure fund/VC roles unless specifically requested.

## Career Stage

- Experienced candidate: lead with total years, current employer, and current scope.
- Student/recent graduate/first short full-time role: lead with graduation cohort and education, then current role.
- For early-career candidates, keep one strongest pre-graduation experience category when names, counts, and project details do not change the match.
- Omit recent self-directed research by default. Include it only when the user explicitly considers its depth or quality a strong signal for the target role; omit it whenever feedback says the thinking is not deep enough.

## Combine Sources

| Source | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Resume | Total years, career timeline, employer, title, responsibility, projects, and metrics |
| Conversation notes | Latest status, opportunity direction, base, motivation, and evidenced working style |
| Both | Use the resume as the factual backbone and the conversation to update or enrich it |

For PDF resumes, extract all pages before drafting. If extraction order is ambiguous, inspect the rendered page. If two sources disagree and recency does not resolve it, omit the fact or add `建议补问`.

## Prioritize Signal

| Priority | Extract | How to write it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Career-stage anchor | Experienced: total years/current role; recent graduate: cohort/education |
| 2 | Named company plus specific business | `在<可公开公司>支持<算法/自动驾驶/新业务团队>` |
| 3 | Target-relevant proof | `高阶算法岗位交付`、`0-1 团队搭建`、`相关人才资源积累` |
| 4 | Other proven capability | `支持组织搭建和关键岗位招聘`、`参与创新业务 0-1 市场营销` |
| 5 | Recipient-relevant opportunity and base | Keep only roles the receiving group can act on |
| 6 | User judgment, conditional | Include when explicitly requested; otherwise it may remain internal context |

Prefer exact, useful facts over broad summaries. Compress chronology into a short equation-like career split rather than replaying the full resume.

When a company name is shareable, use it together with the specific business supported. `在某500强做 HR` is weaker than `在<公司>支持<具体团队/业务>招聘`.

Current scope must be equally specific. If the notes name user growth, operations, search/recommendation, autonomous driving, or another team, do not collapse it to `目前在<公司>负责招聘`.

Industry discussion is not proof of expertise. Write `关注/感兴趣` when the source only shows that the candidate follows a field; write `熟悉/有较深理解` only when direct work, projects, or repeated delivery supports it.

## Tone

- Use direct internal wording and concrete nouns.
- Keep it practical and useful to a busy founder or HRD.
- Avoid formal ratings, long caveats, and interview-report language.
- Use Chinese by default; keep common startup/recruiting terms such as `open` and `base` when they sound natural.
- Keep no more than one evidenced working-style phrase. Drop generic statements about chemistry, business direction, or co-building space.
- Do not force qualitative feedback into the group brief. Negative or ambivalent judgments may guide omission and emphasis without appearing in the final paragraph unless the user explicitly asks to include them.
- Avoid polished but empty phrases such as `可迁移价值`、`有较深理解`、`兼具多元背景`、`经验较完整`、`能力扎实` when a concrete fact can replace them.
- State outcomes only with a concrete result or metric. Otherwise end the clause after the responsibility instead of adding `完成阶段性任务`.
- End with a light CTA: `感兴趣可以私信～`.

## Length

Aim for one paragraph:

- Normal brief: 80-160 Chinese characters.
- Dense senior candidate: up to 200 Chinese characters.
- Do not split into multiple paragraphs unless the user asks for multiple versions.

## Privacy And Redaction

Default redactions:

| Source detail | Safer wording |
|---|---|
| Current sensitive employer name | `某 L4 无人车/自动驾驶相关公司` |
| Prior or public employer when shareable | Keep the company name and duration when they sharpen the candidate's background |
| Exact compensation | `薪酬有弹性` only if the user explicitly wants this and notes support it |
| Contact details | Omit completely |
| Internal reporting conflict | Compress to role preference, not gossip |

Never write `敏感的公司`. Use the industry/category label directly.

## Role Patterns

### HR / Recruiting

Useful evidence:

- Years of in-house HR or recruiting experience.
- Technical, algorithm, AI, robotics, autonomous-driving, infra, or R&D hiring exposure.
- 0-1 team buildout, batch delivery, key/senior role delivery, talent mapping.
- BP or organization-building scope if clearly supported.
- Preference for early-stage HR 一号位, recruiting lead, HRBP, or HR负责人 roles.
- For AI/embodied-intelligence targets: algorithm/R&D hiring, named technical teams or projects, senior technical delivery, and explicitly supported candidate-network depth.

For targeted AI or embodied-intelligence referrals, order HR evidence like this:

1. Current company and current hiring scope.
2. Prior shareable company and specific algorithm/technical business supported.
3. Relevant senior-role delivery and candidate/talent resources when explicitly supported.
4. Startup scaling, BP, or broad HR scope that the target role also needs.

Reusable phrasing:

```text
推荐一位有<总年限>工作经验的 HR 方向候选人，<公司A/岗位X年> + <公司B/招聘方向Y年>，目前在<脱敏公司类型>负责<明确招聘范围>；<一条有直接证据的核心能力>。候选人<一条有证据的工作风格>，open 看<目标岗位>机会，希望 base<城市>，感兴趣可以私信～
```

### Market / Marketing

Useful evidence:

- Media/content background plus company-side marketing experience.
- 0-1 marketing for innovative product lines or new regions.
- Content, entrepreneurship/program planning, campaign execution, event planning.
- Sensitivity to new technology and use of AI to improve marketing workflows.

Reusable phrasing:

```text
推荐一位有<总年限>工作经验的市场方向候选人，<媒体X年> + <公司市场营销Y年>，曾负责<一至两个代表项目>；<一条有直接证据的核心能力>。open 看<目标岗位>机会，希望 base<城市>，感兴趣可以私信～
```

## Drafting Algorithm

1. Parse all sources and reconcile the factual timeline.
2. Decide whether the brief is generic or for a specific company/JD.
3. Identify career stage and choose either an experience-led or cohort/education-led opening.
4. State the current employer/role after that anchor.
5. Add a shareable employer with the specific business supported, or compress early-career internships into one relevant category without counts or names unless they matter.
6. Add no more than two directly evidenced, target-relevant capabilities or resources.
7. Filter opportunity direction by what the recipient group can offer, then add base.
8. Include qualitative judgment only when the user wants it in the group copy; otherwise treat it as selection context.
9. Redact sensitive current-employer and personal details.
10. Remove any phrase that could apply to most candidates without changing meaning.
11. Return only `最终推荐语` unless the source is too thin.

## Calibration Rules

When learning from new samples:

- Add generalized patterns, not the sample itself.
- Convert names and companies to role/category labels.
- Preserve useful phrases that recur across accepted briefs.
- Record what to omit when the final brief deliberately drops raw-note details.
- Keep this guide short enough to read during drafting.
